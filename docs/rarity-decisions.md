# ShipyardIcons v2 rarity assignments

Rarity follows combat history and achievement across the ship's lifetime. Hardware warrants a higher rarity only when it is exceptionally significant. All six ships here are New Orleans-class heavy cruisers; membership in that class alone does not raise their rarity. Their provisional New Orleans combat statistics do not determine rarity.

| Ship | Rarity | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USS Astoria (CA-34) | SR | Participation in the Coral Sea, Midway, and Guadalcanal campaigns before her loss at Savo Island; three WWII battle stars. |
| USS Minneapolis (CA-36) | UR | Seventeen WWII battle stars, service across the major Pacific campaigns, and a return to combat after severe damage at Tassafaronga; comparable overall distinction to the existing UR New Orleans. |
| USS Quincy (CA-39) | R | Atlantic service and support of the Guadalcanal landings, followed by an early loss at Savo Island; one WWII battle star. |
| USS San Francisco (CA-38) | UR | Seventeen WWII battle stars, a Presidential Unit Citation, and prominent service in the Guadalcanal fighting. |
| USS Vincennes (CA-44) | R | Doolittle Raid escort, Midway, and Guadalcanal service before being sunk at Savo Island; two WWII battle stars and a comparatively short combat career. |
| USS Tuscaloosa (CA-37) | SR | Extensive convoy and shore-bombardment service across the Atlantic, Mediterranean, and Pacific, including Normandy, Iwo Jima, and Okinawa; seven WWII battle stars. |

Battle stars support these judgments; they are not a numerical rarity formula. The existing fleet already includes lower-rarity ships, so this artwork batch does not require adding unrelated characters to balance its rarity distribution.
