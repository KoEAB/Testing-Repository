## Assignment rationale

Rarity continues to reflect lifetime service and achievements. These older destroyers do not receive an exceptional-hardware premium simply because of their class or hull type.

- C: Allen, Chew, Kennison, Fox, King, McCormick, Semmes, Dahlgren, Lawrence, MacLeish, Simpson. Their records chiefly reflect local defense, escort, patrol, technical support, or training.
- U: Decatur, Broome, Pruitt, Goldsborough, Hulbert, William B. Preston, Barker, Bulmer, Babbitt, Badger, Stribling, Satterlee. These represent more operational escort, minesweeping, aviation-support, or early-war frontline service without a major individual fleet-battle distinction.
- R: Cole, Greer, Elliot, Stringham, Little, Gregory, Manley, Dent, Dickerson. These include hazardous amphibious service, the Safi assault, or the historically significant Greer incident. Destroyer-sized hardware gives no automatic promotion into the highest rarities.

These are tier judgments, not a claim that every ship within a tier had identical achievements. Existing higher-rarity ships were not reassigned merely to change the distribution.
