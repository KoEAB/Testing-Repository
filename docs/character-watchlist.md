## Matching and version distinctions

Ignore capitalization, incidental spacing, and unambiguous navy prefixes when matching. Recognize hyphen/spacing variants of hull codes and names such as Z43/Z-43. Do not discard II, META, CV, or µ qualifiers: these distinguish characters. Treat the micro sign µ and Greek letter μ as equivalent for Noshiro's listed variant.

| Listed character | Also warn for | Distinct original, not matched by this entry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laffey II | USS Laffey (DD-724), DD-724 | USS Laffey (DD-459) |
| Lexington II | USS Lexington (CV-16), CV-16 | USS Lexington (CV-2) |

II means the second ship with that name. If a proposed name omits a distinguishing qualifier and the hull or version is unclear, resolve the identity before assuming a match. For example, the existing USS Hornet does not become Hornet META, and Amagi (CV) does not mean the battlecruiser Amagi.
